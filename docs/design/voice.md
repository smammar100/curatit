# Voice and copy

Curatit talks to designers: practical, specific, never hyped.

## Rules

- **Sentence case everywhere**: buttons, headings, labels, menu items. Never Title Case.
- **Short declarative sentences.** Heroes often stack two: "You saved it. Somewhere. / Now it's
  one search away."
- **Subtext under a heading is two lines at most.**
- **Buttons say the outcome**: "Create board", "Copy link", "Save changes". Never "Submit", "OK"
  or "Click here". Every sign-up action reads "Create account".
- **Errors say what happened and what to do**: "Add the part after the @, like
  ammar@studio.com." Never "Invalid input" or "Something went wrong".
- **Empty states say what's empty, why, and the next step**, with one action.
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks** (rare and playful at most), no "please", no "simply".
- **Numbers, not adjectives**: "8 or more characters", not "a strong password".
- **British spelling**, as the site uses it: colour, recognise, organise.
- Use `…` (the character), curly quotes and apostrophes in copy (`&rsquo;` in JSX).

```
Correct:   "This email is already in use." [Sign in] [Contact support]
Incorrect: "Oops! Something Went Wrong. Please Try Again!"
```
